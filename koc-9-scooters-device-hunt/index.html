The writing question

Before you write: choose 2 devices that best answer the question. Choose smartly.
  • Pick devices with a strong key word you can explain in step 3.
  • A strong pair shows two sides: one device about how fast, careless and dangerous the scooters are, and one about the people who are hurt and the people who support the writer.
Reminders
  • Daniel Marsh is a man: write he or Marsh, never “she.”
  • Evidence: “First, Marsh claims that …” (or warns that / states that / describes … as / asks), then copy the words exactly inside quotation marks. Never write the word “quote.”
  • In step 3, give the meaning of ONE word in ONE sentence.
  • In step 4, use the frame for YOUR device, then write “Marsh highlights …”.
  • Step 5: “The reader is … because ….” first, then “The reader realizes ….”
  • Step 6 for BOTH devices: “Therefore, the (device) shows Marsh’s bias towards ….”
  • Never start a sentence with “To sum up,” “To conclude” or “All in all.”
  • Each box gets a gold star when every part of its structure is there. The ticks check structure; the 💡 hints help with ideas.
