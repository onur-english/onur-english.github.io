The introduction (in the exam, it is given)

① Choose 2 devices that directly connect to the thesis in the introduction. Choose smartly.
  • The thesis is the last sentence: a history chosen by the powerful can hide people's identity, and people must discover their own history for themselves.
  • A strong pair: one device about the history he WAS taught (the opening, Dick Whittington) and one about a hero he was NOT taught (Toussaint, Nanny or Mary Seacole).
