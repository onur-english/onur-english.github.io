The introduction (in the exam, it is given)

① Choose 2 devices that directly connect to the thesis in the introduction. Choose smartly.
  • The thesis is the last sentence: racism and hatred cannot break the speaker, because she rises above them with pride, confidence and hope.
  • A strong pair: one device about the hate she faces (lines 13–24) and one about how she rises (lines 1–12 or 29–43).
