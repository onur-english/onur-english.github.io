How it works

01Answer every question in a level. Missed questions come back in the next round until you get them right.

02In typing questions, check your spelling. Capital letters don't matter. Short forms like hadn't and long forms like had not are both fine.

03Your first-try score fills the dots: 90% or more = 3 dots, 70% or more = 2 dots, finished = 1 dot. Get 3 dots everywhere before the exam.

04Correct answers in a row build your streak and earn XP (replaying a question you already cleared earns 1 XP).

05Vocabulary questions show a short animation of the word's meaning. Start with Learn the Words (flashcards) and Picture the Word. Grammar levels have a worked example and a rule animation. The Mock Exam has no pictures, like the real exam.

06After two misses the app shows you the answer, and it always tells you why your answer was wrong.
