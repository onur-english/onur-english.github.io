① Choose 2 devices that directly connect to the thesis in the introduction. Choose smartly.
  • The thesis is the last sentence: the rat, tough, unwanted and impossible to destroy, deserves a poem as much as any noble creature.
  • A strong pair: one device about how the rat is seen and treated and one about the rat's toughness and pride.
