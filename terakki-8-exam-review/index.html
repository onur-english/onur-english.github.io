How it works

01Every track is open. Play them in any order. Missed questions come back in the next round until you get them right.

02Typing: capital letters don't matter, and short forms like hadn't and long forms like had not are both fine. Check your spelling.

03Sentence completion and reading answers are checked for the right tense and the key information. After each one, compare your answer with the full-mark model.

04Writing tracks: tap a line of the text to open a box right under it. The bar at the bottom counts your words. Copied phrases are flagged, so use your own words.

05Your first-try score fills the dots: 90% or more = 3 dots, 70% or more = 2. Get 3 dots everywhere before the exam.
