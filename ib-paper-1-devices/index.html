Spot every device

Paper 1 texts are full of persuasive features. Learn the 18 you will meet most often, then train your eye until you can spot each one in seconds, ending with real speeches, posters and slogans.

  1. Learn: read each device card, with a real example.
  2. Spot it: 54 practice sentences. Name the device in the highlighted words.
  3. Twins: 12 rounds on devices that students mix up.
  4. Field test: 18 real texts from history, politics and advertising.

A wrong answer comes back in the next round, with the options moved around, until you get it right. Your first-try score is your real score. Practice sentences in Spot it and Twins were written for this app; every example marked “real” is quoted from its actual source.
