A real 2024 anti-smoking print ad from the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Decode it, learn the moves that take an answer to the top band, then write a full Paper 1 answer with the writing boxes opening right inside the ad.

Guiding question

How does the advertisement use visual and written features to expose the tobacco industry and persuade smokers to quit?

The ad: a 2024 print ad from the CDC’s “Tips From Former Smokers” campaign, featuring Angie P. It is a US government work in the public domain. The campaign: launched in March 2012, it was the first federally funded national tobacco education campaign. It shows real people living with the effects of smoking. Angie: according to the CDC, she started smoking menthol cigarettes at 15, after watching her mother smoke. Menthol: the CDC has reported that the tobacco industry aggressively marketed menthol products to young people and African Americans, and that African American adults have the highest rate of menthol cigarette use.
