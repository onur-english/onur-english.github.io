CASE FILE 01

The Road Safety Poster

A public awareness poster is trying to change how people drive. Twelve clues are marked on it: some are words, some are parts of the image. Find out what each one does, then write an IB-style analysis paragraph.

Guiding question

How does the poster use visual and written features to persuade drivers not to use their phones while driving?

  1. 1Investigate. Tap each numbered clue on the poster. Name the feature, then choose what it does to the viewer.
  2. 2Sharpen. Choose the stronger sentence for each move of a Paper 1 paragraph.
  3. 3Write. Build your own analysis paragraph with the sentence frames. The desk checks your structure as you type, then shows you a model answer.
Why this matters for Paper 1 In Paper 1 you analyze an unseen non-literary text, such as a poster, an advertisement or an infographic. SL students choose one of two texts and have 1 hour 15 minutes. HL students write about both texts in 2 hours 15 minutes. The skills you practice here are the same at both levels.
