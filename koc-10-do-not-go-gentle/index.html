The introduction (in the exam, it is given)

① Choose 2 devices that directly connect to the thesis in the introduction. Choose smartly.
  • The thesis is the last sentence: Thomas urges his father, and all people, to fight death with passion instead of accepting it quietly.
  • A strong pair: one device about the call to fight death (lines 1–3 and the repeated refrains) and one about the men who fight or the father (lines 4–19).
