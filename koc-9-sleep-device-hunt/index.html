The writing question

Before you write: choose 2 devices that best answer the question. Choose smartly.
  • Pick devices with a strong key word you can explain in step 3.
  • A strong pair: one device that makes early starts look harmful, and one that makes the reader feel something (sympathy, anger or fear).
Reminders
  • Elena Ward is a woman: write she or Ward, never “he.”
  • Evidence: “First, Ward claims that …” (or warns that / states that / describes … as / asks), then copy the words exactly inside quotation marks. Never write the word “quote.”
  • In step 3, give the meaning of ONE word in ONE sentence.
  • In step 4, use the frame for YOUR device. If the source is not named, say so.
  • Never start a sentence with “To sum up,” “To conclude” or “All in all.”
  • Each box gets a gold star when every part of its structure is there. The ticks check structure; the 💡 hints help with ideas.
