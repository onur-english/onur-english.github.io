The introduction (in the exam, it is given)

① Choose 2 devices that directly connect to the thesis in the introduction. Choose smartly.
  • The thesis is the last sentence: everything in the world is connected and always changing, and the cloud's freedom is something precious that not everyone can share.
  • A strong pair: one device about the cloud's many past lives (lines 1–26) and one about the prisoners watching it (lines 27–31).
