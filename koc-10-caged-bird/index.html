The introduction (in the exam, it is given)

① Choose 2 devices that directly connect to the thesis in the introduction. Choose smartly.
  • The thesis is the last sentence: oppressed people are denied the freedom that others take for granted, but they never stop longing and singing for freedom.
  • A strong pair: one device about what the caged bird has lost (lines 8–14 or 27–30) and one about its song or the free bird it is compared with.
