The introduction (in the exam, it is given)

① Choose 2 devices that directly connect to the thesis in the introduction. Choose smartly.
  • The thesis is the last sentence: every choice in life means giving something up, and people later give their choices a meaning they did not have at the time.
  • A strong pair: one device about how hard and uncertain a choice is and one about the meaning people give their choices later.
