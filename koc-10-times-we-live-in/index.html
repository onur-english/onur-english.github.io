These Are the Times

Imtiaz Dharker, 2006. Three vaults, seven locks, one exit. Keep your Poetry Book open at page 22.

  • Each lock has two dials. Dial 1: name the device by picking the statement that is true. Dial 2: choose why it works. Read every option carefully: they are all about the same words.
  • A wrong answer trips the alarm. The guard re-locks that lock and brings it back in the next pass, with the answers moved around.
  • Open every lock, then crack each vault's code. The exit only opens when all three vaults are open.
  • Your first-try score is your real score. Getting out is the goal.

You have a heist in progress.
