The Bosses of the Senate

A real political cartoon by Joseph Keppler, printed in Puck magazine on January 23, 1889. Twelve clues are pinned on it. Work out what each one does, then write an IB-style analysis paragraph.

Guiding question

How does the cartoonist use visual and written features to criticize the power of big business over the government?

Context you need A trust was a group of companies joined together to control a whole industry, such as oil or sugar, so they could set prices with no competition. A monopolist is a person or company that controls all of a product or service. The Senate is part of the United States Congress. It makes the country’s laws. Abraham Lincoln described American democracy in 1863 as “government of the people, by the people, for the people.” Look for an echo of these words on the cartoon. A year after this cartoon, in 1890, Congress passed the Sherman Antitrust Act, a law against monopolies.
